# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Design

- Goals/non-goals.
- Product benchmark.
- Architecture and boundaries.
- State/dependency/retry/cancellation/recovery semantics.
- Product UX.
- Contract design.
- Phase-0 review and ADR acceptance.

## Phase 1 — Core product

Only after the Design Gate:

- semantic kernel,
- durable execution state,
- reconciler,
- execution adapter contract,
- API/CLI,
- operational UI,
- recovery operations.

## Phase 2 — Advanced orchestration

Potential additions after real use validates them:

- dynamic expansion,
- subworkflows,
- event waits,
- caching/memoization,
- richer scoped setup/finalizers,
- Open Workflow Specification importer,
- OpenLineage adapter,
- multi-region/multi-control-plane architecture.
